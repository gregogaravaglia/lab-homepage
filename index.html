27 countries and counting

As someone who grew up between a ranch and a small town in the middle of the Argentine countryside, I love to travel and experience new places.

Traveling has allowed me to experience different cultures, meet people from around the world, and see places that I had previously only seen in pictures.

These are some of the trips that impressed me the most.

  • Bucharest, Romania

    Some of the best nightlife I've experienced. I also got to visit Dracula's castle in Bran.

  • Mexicali, Mexico

    I've been here three times, all of them as part of a Lowe Institute team collecting data on refugee migration trends and the economic integration of immigrants to the U.S. Unique experience.

  • Auschwitz, Poland

    I visited the concentration camp and other memorial sites in neighboring Krakow.

  • Reykjavik, Iceland

    This was the furthest north I've ever been. I would love to go back to Iceland (with more money this time) to visit the more remote parts of the island.

You can find the rest of my trips on my Instagram page
