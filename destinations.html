Some of my favorite destinations

Traveling has taken me to many different countries and cities. Every destination has been different, which is one of the reasons I enjoy traveling so much.

I particularly enjoy places where I can experience a different culture and explore somewhere that feels different from what I know.

Here are a few destinations that stand out from my travels so far.

Mexico

Mexico has been an important destination for me because I have visited multiple times. My trips to Mexicali were connected to research with the Lowe Institute.

I also went to Cabo San Lucas for Spring Break, which as you can imagine, was a completely opposite experience.

Mexico is also interesting because different parts of the country offer very different realities.

Argentina

This might be a little biased because this is my home country, but I genuinely think that Argentina is one of the best places you could ever visit. I personally think that Buenos Aires is one of the best cities in the world, but that's not all we have to offer. Argentina has glaciers, snow-capped mountains where you can ski, green mountains where you can hike, deserts, beaches, dunes, jungles, and on top of breathtaking landscapes, it's a very welcoming country where you can make friends even if you barely speak Spanish. Few other places will try to make a foreigner feel at home the way Argentina and its people do.

Having grown up in the Argentine countryside, returning to the country also gives me the opportunity to reconnect with places that I visited as a child and teenager, like the ski town of Bariloche, or the Iguazu falls.

At the same time, traveling around Argentina has shown me how different regions of the country can be. So far, I've been to 13 out of 23 provinces, plus the federal district of Buenos Aires.

Iceland

As of now, Reykjavik is the northernmost places I have visited.

One of the things I liked most about Iceland was the feeling that there was still a lot left to explore outside the main city.

I would like to return someday and spend more time exploring the remote parts of the island.

I still have many more destinations to explore.
